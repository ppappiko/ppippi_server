# SD pet renderer + fox pilot model

Shared conventions (both steps MUST follow these exactly):
- Pet models are Blockbench `.bbmodel` files (format_version 4.x, model_format "free", box_uv false) stored at `dev/pipi-facilities/assets/pipifacilities/pets/<skin>.bbmodel`, loaded in game as resource `pipifacilities:pets/<skin>.bbmodel`. The texture is embedded as base64 PNG in `textures[0].source` (`data:image/png;base64,...`); `resolution.width/height` give the UV pixel space (64x64 for new pets).
- Units are pixels (16 = 1 block). The model's front faces -Z (north); feet stand on y=0; centered on x=0,z=0.
- Bones are `outliner` groups: `{name, uuid, origin:[x,y,z], rotation:[x,y,z], visibility, children:[element uuid strings or nested groups]}`. Elements: `{name, uuid, type:"cube", from, to, origin, rotation:[x,y,z] (optional), inflate (optional), faces:{north,south,east,west,up,down:{uv:[u1,v1,u2,v2], texture:0, rotation (0/90/180/270, optional)}}}`. Rotations are Euler degrees applied in Blockbench order (Z, then Y, then X on the PoseStack, i.e. mulPose(ZP) mulPose(YP) mulPose(XP)) around the element/group origin.
- Animations: `animations:[{name, loop:"loop"|"once"|"hold", length (seconds), animators:{<group uuid>:{name, type:"bone", keyframes:[{channel:"rotation"|"position"|"scale", time, interpolation:"linear"|"catmullrom", data_points:[{x,y,z}]}]}}}]`. data_points values may be numbers or numeric strings. Blockbench convention when applied: rotation adds (-x, -y, +z) degrees to the rest rotation; position adds (-x, +y, +z) pixels; scale multiplies (default 1).
- Animation names used by the game: `idle`, `walk`, `work`, `eat`, `hurt`. Groups whose name ends with `_work` are visible only during `work`; `_eat` only during `eat`. Elements whose name starts with `hitbox` are never drawn.

## s1 Data-driven bbmodel pet renderer
files: dev/pipi-facilities/src/PetModels.java, dev/pipi-facilities/src/PetRenderers.java, dev/pipi-facilities/build.sh
difficulty: hard
do: Add a client-side loader `PetModels` that lazily reads `pipifacilities:pets/<skin>.bbmodel` through `Minecraft.getInstance().getResourceManager()`, parses it with Gson (bundled with Minecraft) into bones/cubes/animations following the shared conventions, registers the embedded PNG as a `DynamicTexture` (NativeImage.read) with the TextureManager under `pipifacilities:dynamic/pet_<skin>`, and caches the result per skin (a missing or broken file caches "absent" and logs one warning). In `PetRenderers.Renderer.render`, when a model exists for the skin, draw it instead of the hard-coded cubes: same outer pose as now (`mulPose(YP 180-yaw)`, scale 1.014/16), walk the bone tree applying rest + animated transforms, and emit each cube's 6 faces with their own UVs divided by the resolution, using standard Minecraft/Blockbench face orientation (north face seen from -Z has u1 at +X; south u1 at -X; east u1 at +Z; west u1 at -Z; side faces v1 at top; up u1 at -X/v1 at -Z; down u1 at -X/v1 at +Z), honoring face uv rotation, transformed normals, `entityCutoutNoCull`, light and hurt overlay. Animation choice: action 0 idle, 1 walk (also when action 0 and walkAnimation.speed(partial) > .015), 2 work, 3 eat; while `hurtTime > 0` play `hurt` if present. Time = (tickCount + partial) / 20 s; loop wraps by length, once/hold clamp. Keyframes interpolate linearly per channel (treat catmullrom as linear is fine). Crossfade 0.2 s from the previous animation's pose to the new one when the animation changes (keep per-entity state in a WeakHashMap keyed by the entity). Skins without a bbmodel keep the existing hard-coded cube rendering exactly as it is today — do not delete or alter that path. Also write `dev/pipi-facilities/build.sh` (bash, runs from repo root) that compiles `dev/pipi-facilities/src/*.java` with `javac --release 21 -proc:none -encoding UTF-8` and classpath = `C:/Users/user/Documents/Codex/2026-11-04/sor/work/hud-minecraft-named.jar` plus every `*.jar` under `C:/Users/user/Documents/Codex/2026-11-04/sor/work/gradle-cache/caches/modules-2` except names containing `sources`, `srgutils`, `-all.jar`, `fatjar`, `userdev` (joined with `;`), then unpacks the newest non-.bak `mods/PipiFacilities-*.jar` into a temp dir, overwrites `dev/pipi/facilities/*.class` with the fresh classes and copies `dev/pipi-facilities/assets/` over `assets/`, bumps the patch version in `META-INF/neoforge.mods.toml` (e.g. 1.0.13 -> 1.0.14), and writes `dev/pipi-facilities/out/PipiFacilities-<newversion>.jar` with the JDK `jar` tool. It must not modify anything under `mods/`.
dont: change PipiPets.java, entity sync data, sounds or routes; touch mods/ or plugins/.

## s2 SD fox pilot model generator
files: dev/pipi-pets/build-fox.mjs, dev/pipi-pets/lib/bbmodel.mjs, dev/pipi-facilities/assets/pipifacilities/pets/fox.bbmodel, dev/pipi-pets/out/fox.png
difficulty: hard
do: Write a Node (ESM, no npm dependencies; use built-in `zlib` for PNG encoding) generator. `lib/bbmodel.mjs` holds reusable helpers: a 64x64 RGBA canvas with fillRect/pixel, a PNG encoder, a UV packer that gives every cube face its own island (per-face UVs, not box UV), a per-face painter that fills a face with a base color plus soft shading (up face lighter, down face darker, sides mid; 1px slightly darker rim on side-face bottom edges; light 1-2 shade dithering), cube/group/animation builders that emit the shared bbmodel conventions with random uuids, and a writer. `build-fox.mjs` builds "빨간 후드 여우" (red-hooded fox) in a cute SD / chibi style and writes `dev/pipi-facilities/assets/pipifacilities/pets/fox.bbmodel` plus `dev/pipi-pets/out/fox.png` (the texture alone for review). Proportions: total height about 14 px; big head about 10 wide x 9 tall x 8 deep sitting on a small body about 6 wide x 5 tall x 7 deep; short stubby legs about 2x3x2; big fluffy tail about 4x4x8 angled up behind; pointy ears built from 2 stacked shrinking cubes each (orange outside, cream inside front face); short snout 4x2x2 on the face with a 2x1 dark nose. Head front face painting: two 2x2 dark brown (#2B1D18) eyes with a 1px white highlight in the upper-left pixel, pink blush 2x1 (#F59AA0) under each eye, cream (#FFF1DC) lower face. Palette: fur #E8823A, fur shade #C4652A, fur light #F6A866, cream #FFF1DC, paws/ear tips/tail tip dark #5A3A2A for paws and cream for tail tip, hood red #C8323C, hood shade #9A2230, hood light #E35A62, small gold clasp #F2C14E. The red hood is a slightly inflated (about 0.25-0.5) shell over the top/back of the head with the ears poking through (hood cubes leave the ears visible) and a short cape cube draped over the upper back. Bones: `root` (0,0,0) > `body` (origin 0,4,0) > `head` (origin 0,7,-2, contains `ear_l`, `ear_r`, `hood`, `snout`), `tail` (origin 0,6,3.5), `leg_fl`, `leg_fr`, `leg_bl`, `leg_br` (origins at the leg tops). Animations (loop unless noted): `idle` 2.0 s gentle breathing (body scale y 1->1.03->1, head bob 0.3 px, tail sway ±10° yaw, one quick ear twitch); `walk` 0.6 s legs swing ±35° pitch in diagonal pairs, body bob 0.5 px, head counter-bob, tail sway ±15°; `work` 1.2 s busy crafting: head nods down 15° twice, front legs alternately raise and tap 40°, tail wag fast; `eat` 0.8 s head dips 25° nibbling twice, tail slow wag; `hurt` 0.3 s once: body squash (scale y 0.85, x/z 1.1) then recover, head tilts back 15°.
dont: add npm packages; touch dev/pipi-facilities/src or anything outside the listed files.
done-when: `node dev/pipi-pets/build-fox.mjs` exits 0 and `node -e "const d=JSON.parse(require('fs').readFileSync('dev/pipi-facilities/assets/pipifacilities/pets/fox.bbmodel','utf8'));if(d.resolution.width!==64||!d.textures[0].source.startsWith('data:image/png;base64,')||!['idle','walk','work','eat','hurt'].every(n=>d.animations.some(a=>a.name===n)))process.exit(1)"` exits 0.
