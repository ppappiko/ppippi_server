# Smooth keyframe interpolation + high-quality fox animations

Model/animation conventions are those of dev/plans/sd-pets.md "Shared conventions". The in-game renderer is dev/pipi-facilities/src/PetModels.java; the offline preview dev/pipi-pets/preview.mjs mirrors its math and must stay in sync with it.

## s1 Catmull-Rom and step interpolation in renderer and preview
files: dev/pipi-facilities/src/PetModels.java, dev/pipi-pets/preview.mjs
difficulty: medium
do: Both files currently interpolate keyframes linearly. Parse each keyframe's `interpolation` field and evaluate Blockbench-compatible: for the segment between keyframe A (before t) and B (after t), if A is `step` hold A's value; else if A or B is `catmullrom` (treat `bezier` the same) use a uniform Catmull-Rom spline through P0, A, B, P3 where P0 is the keyframe before A and P3 the keyframe after B (if missing, reuse A or B; but in a `loop` animation whose first and last keyframes sit at 0 and length, wrap around: the neighbour before the first is the second-to-last and the neighbour after the last is the second), with local parameter s = (t - tA) / (tB - tA), per channel component x/y/z; otherwise linear. Before the first keyframe hold the first value, after the last hold the last. Keep everything else (conventions, crossfade, visibility) unchanged, and keep the Java and JS implementations equivalent (same neighbour and wrap rules).
done-when: `bash dev/pipi-facilities/build.sh` exits 0 and `node dev/pipi-pets/preview.mjs fox walk` exits 0.

## s2 High-quality fox animations
files: dev/pipi-pets/lib/bbmodel.mjs, dev/pipi-pets/build-fox.mjs, dev/pipi-facilities/assets/pipifacilities/pets/fox.bbmodel, dev/pipi-pets/out/fox.png
difficulty: medium
do: In lib/bbmodel.mjs make `keyframes(channel, keys, interp = "linear")` backward compatible: every emitted keyframe gets `interpolation: interp`, and a key array may carry an optional 5th element string overriding it for that key (e.g. "step" or "linear"). Then rewrite only the animation section of build-fox.mjs (keep geometry and textures exactly as they are) using `"catmullrom"` for organic motion, with the principles of good game animation: easing in/out, anticipation before big moves, overlapping/follow-through (tail and ears lag the body by a fraction of the cycle), and loops whose first and last keys are identical. Existing sign conventions in build-fox.mjs stay valid (positive X rotation on the head = nod down; legs swing as the current walk). Targets: `idle` 4 s — breathing (body scale y 1 → 1.02 → 1 twice), a slow look-around (head yaw 0 → 12° by 1.0 s, hold, back to 0 by 2.2 s, -8° at 3.0 s, 0 at 4.0 s) with a slight head tilt following it, a quick right-ear twitch around 2.5 s (fast in and out, use linear for that key), tail slowly swaying ±8° yaw lagging the head by about 0.3 s; `walk` 0.8 s — legs in diagonal pairs ±35° pitch, body bob 0.4 px down at each foot contact (twice per cycle), body roll ±2°, head counter-bob and ±2° pitch, tail yaw ±10° lagging a quarter cycle, ears swaying slightly back on each step; `work` 1.2 s — digging: body pitched 5° forward and head 25° down held through the loop, front legs alternately scrape with a fast backward swipe to 60° (about 0.15 s) and a slower return (about 0.45 s), tail raised about 25° and wagging ±15° yaw quickly, ears perked; `eat` 1.6 s — head eases down to 40° over 0.3 s, two chew dips of 5° around 0.6 s and 0.9 s, lifts 10° at 1.3 s and returns down to 40° at 1.6 s so the loop is seamless with the start of the dip held, tail slow wag; `hurt` 0.4 s once — immediate flinch at 0 s (head pitched up 20°, body squash scale x/z 1.08 y 0.9, ears flattened back about 30°), overshoot past rest at about 0.25 s, settle to rest at 0.4 s.
done-when: `node dev/pipi-pets/build-fox.mjs` exits 0 and `node dev/pipi-pets/check.mjs fox` exits 0.
