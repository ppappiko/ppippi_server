# Vanilla-style pet pipeline + fox redesign

Background: the user rejected the SD look (big flat-shaded boxes, every pet the same box-body-on-4-legs shape). The new direction is "fantasy creatures that look like real Minecraft vanilla mobs": integer-pixel boxes, textures at exactly 1 texel per model pixel with vanilla-style hand-placed pixel art and noisy multi-tone fur (no smooth gradients, no stretched texels), vanilla mob proportions (quadrupeds have a long horizontal body with the head in front, not a cube on legs).

Model format, coordinates and animation conventions are unchanged from dev/plans/sd-pets.md "Shared conventions" (bbmodel, front faces -Z, feet at y=0, rotation order Z then Y then X, keyframe rotation applied as (-x,-y,+z), position as (-x,+y,+z)). The in-game renderer is dev/pipi-facilities/src/PetModels.java and is the source of truth for face UV orientation and transforms.

## s1 Pixel-art model library
files: dev/pipi-pets/lib/vanilla.mjs
difficulty: medium
do: Write a new library (leave lib/bbmodel.mjs untouched; you may import uuid/hex/encodePNG/Canvas from it). It provides a `VBuilder({width,height})` (64x64 default, grows to 128 if needed) with: `material(name, [[hex, weight], ...])` defining a weighted noise palette (deterministic per-texel hash, so builds are reproducible); `cube(name, from, to, {mat, faces, inflate})` where from/to are integers, each face gets its own UV rect sized exactly to the face (width x height in pixels, 1 texel per pixel; inflate does not change UV size), packed without overlap; `faces` maps a face name (north/south/east/west/up/down) to either a material name, an ASCII grid (array of equal-length strings, one char per texel, using `legend` chars mapped to material names or literal hex colors, '.' = transparent), or a function (x, y, w, h) returning a material name or hex. Grid row 0 is the top of the face and column 0 the left as seen from outside the cube; for `up` row 0 is the north (-Z) edge and column 0 is -X; for `down` row 0 is the south (+Z) edge and column 0 is -X — the painter must place texels so that this matches how PetModels.java samples each face (read that file and mirror its per-face vertex/UV order; write a short comment documenting the mapping). Also `group(name, origin, children, {rotation})`, `animation(name, loop, length, animators)` with the same keyframe helper shape as lib/bbmodel.mjs, and `write(bbmodelPath, pngPath, modelName)` that emits the bbmodel (embedded base64 PNG, resolution = texture size) and the PNG.
done-when: `node -e "import('./dev/pipi-pets/lib/vanilla.mjs').then(m=>{if(typeof m.VBuilder!=='function')process.exit(1)})"` exits 0.

## s2 Offline preview renderer
files: dev/pipi-pets/preview.mjs
difficulty: hard
do: Write a Node ESM software renderer (no npm deps; PNG via zlib or reuse encodePNG from lib/bbmodel.mjs, PNG decode of the embedded texture via zlib inflate supporting 8-bit RGBA/RGB non-interlaced with all 5 filter types) used as `node dev/pipi-pets/preview.mjs <skin> [animation] [timeSeconds]`. It loads dev/pipi-facilities/assets/pipifacilities/pets/<skin>.bbmodel, evaluates the bone tree pose at the given animation/time (default: rest pose) using exactly the transform math and face UV mapping of dev/pipi-facilities/src/PetModels.java (read it and mirror it: rest rotation + keyframe conventions, rotation order, origins, inflate, per-face UV corners, `_work`/`_eat` visibility, hitbox skipping), and rasterizes textured triangles with a z-buffer, nearest-neighbour sampling, alpha-cutout, and Minecraft-like face light (up 1.0, north/south 0.8, east/west 0.6, down 0.5, computed from the posed normal). It writes dev/pipi-pets/out/<skin>-preview.png: a 1024x384 image on a light gray background with three orthographic views side by side — front three-quarter (camera yaw 35° to the pet's front-left, pitch 20° down), pure side (pet facing left), back three-quarter — each auto-fit so the model fills about 80% of its panel, with a 1px darker floor line at y=0. When an animation is given, also write dev/pipi-pets/out/<skin>-<animation>-strip.png with 6 evenly spaced frames across the animation length from the front three-quarter view (each 256x256).
done-when: `node dev/pipi-pets/preview.mjs fox` exits 0 and writes dev/pipi-pets/out/fox-preview.png, and `node dev/pipi-pets/preview.mjs fox walk` exits 0 and writes dev/pipi-pets/out/fox-walk-strip.png.

## s3 Vanilla-style fox 빨간 후드 여우
files: dev/pipi-pets/build-fox.mjs, dev/pipi-facilities/assets/pipifacilities/pets/fox.bbmodel, dev/pipi-pets/out/fox.png
needs: s1
difficulty: medium
do: Rewrite build-fox.mjs on top of lib/vanilla.mjs (replace the SD version entirely). Geometry in integer pixels, front -Z: legs 2x5x2 at x -3..-1 / 1..3 and z -4..-2 (front) / 3..5 (back), y 0..5, pivots at their tops; body x -3..3, y 5..11, z -5..6 (a long horizontal fox body); head x -4..4, y 8..14, z -10..-4, pivot (0,10,-5); snout x -2..2, y 8..10, z -13..-10 inside the head bone; ears 2x3x1 at x -4..-2 and 2..4, y 14..17, z -8..-7 in bones ear_l/ear_r under head; hood: a red shell x -4..4, y 9..14, z -8..-4 with inflate 0.5 in the head bone (it frames the face and covers the back of the head; ears poke out of its top); cape x -4..4, y 7..11, z -4..1 with inflate 0.25 in the body bone (a short cloak over the shoulders); tail x -2..2, y 6..10, z 6..15 in bone `tail` pivot (0,10,6) with a rest rotation that makes it droop about 20° downward toward its tip. Materials: fur [["#E07A2E",5],["#D06A22",3],["#EA8C3E",2],["#C45E1C",1]]; white [["#F4EEE4",4],["#E9E0D2",2],["#FFFFFF",1]]; dark [["#3A2A24",4],["#2E211C",2],["#4A362D",2]]; red [["#B3262D",5],["#9C1F26",3],["#C8343A",2]]; hem [["#7A161C",1]]; gold [["#E3B23C",2],["#C99A2A",1]]; eye "#1E1512". Faces: head north grid rows top to bottom "oooooooo","oooooooo","owkookwo","wwooooww","wwwwwwww","wwwwwwww" (o fur, w white, k eye); head down white; snout north "wkkw","wwww", snout up fur, snout sides and down white; ear north "kk","ow","ow", other ear faces fur with dark top row; body up fur, body down white, body sides fur with the bottom row white; legs: top 2 rows fur, bottom 3 rows dark on all sides, down dark; tail: fur, with the last 3 texels toward the tip (+Z end) white on side/up/down faces and the south face white; hood: red with the bottom row of every side face hem; cape: red with the bottom row alternating gold/hem on side faces. Animations, vanilla-like and subtle: `idle` 3 s loop (tail sway ±6° yaw, one ear twitch at 2.2 s, head tilt 3°); `walk` 0.66 s loop (legs ±40° pitch in diagonal pairs, tail sway ±8°, no body bob); `work` 1.0 s loop (digging: head pitched down 20°, front legs alternately scrape back 50°, tail raised); `eat` 0.8 s loop (head down 35° with two small chew dips); `hurt` 0.3 s once (head flinches up 15°, ears back). Use the fox-style sign conventions already proven in the old build-fox.mjs (positive keyframe X on the head = nod down; legs swing as in its walk).
done-when: `node dev/pipi-pets/build-fox.mjs` exits 0 and `node dev/pipi-pets/check.mjs fox` exits 0.
