## s2 Plugin: resource node behaviour, recipes, cores, shop, capacity
files: dev/pipi-rpg/src, plugins/PipiLife/config.yml
difficulty: hard
do: New class ResourceNodes (listener registered by the plugin), working in every world, on the mod blocks PIPIFACILITIES_AMBER_ROCK / _ROCK_PILE / _REDWOOD_TREE_LOG / _REDWOOD_TREE_LEAVES / _CABBAGE_CROP (resolve with Material.matchMaterial, skip gracefully if missing). Creative-mode players break/place normally (building). Survival: breaking amber_rock is cancelled, drops 1 호박석 (mod amber) plus a 3% chance of 1 호박석 코어 (plus the pickaxe enchant core option bonus like the other cores), turns the block into rock_pile and schedules it back to amber_rock after `resource-nodes.amber-respawn-seconds` (300); rock_pile and leaves can't be broken in survival. Breaking a redwood_tree_log with any axe fells the whole connected tree at once (BFS over touching redwood logs and leaves, cap 300 blocks), drops one 홍서나무 원목 (mod redwood_log) per log block at the base, records the tree's blocks with their BlockData and restores them after `resource-nodes.tree-respawn-seconds` (600); without an axe the break is cancelled with "도끼로 베어야 합니다.". Breaking a cabbage_crop drops 1 양배추 씨앗 (mod cabbage_seed), removes it, and restores it after `resource-nodes.cabbage-respawn-seconds` (600). Pending restores are saved to plugins/PipiLife/resource-nodes.yml (world, xyz, block data string, due time) and replayed on enable (overdue ones immediately); a restore skips a spot that is no longer air/rock_pile (don't overwrite builds). Add the config keys with Korean comments.
